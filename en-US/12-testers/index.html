Day-14 Review

Nailing Google's 7 Production Access Questions

Once your 12 testers for 14 days run wraps up, Google asks 7 questions before unlocking production. Shipping a mid-test update (like v1.0.1) plus crisp answers is the #1 approval signal.

  • 1. How easy was it to recruit testers for your app? Explain how you organized a structured cohort of 25+ real Android users across diverse devices.
  • 2. How did testers engage with your app during closed testing? Highlight daily app opens, core feature walkthroughs, and steady 14-day retention.
  • 3. What feedback did you receive from your 12 testers? Summarize UI/UX suggestions, performance notes, and private Play Console feedback.
  • 4. Who is your intended target audience? Define your core user base and how your closed testing group mirrored real-world users.
  • 5. What value does your app provide to users? Point to key features, stability proven during testing, and the exact problem your app solves.
  • 6. What changes did you make based on tester feedback? Cite the mid-test update (e.g., v1.0.1) you shipped to resolve layout or crash bugs.
  • 7. How did you decide your app is ready for production? Reference zero crashes, clean Android Vitals metrics, and 14 days of resolved feedback.
