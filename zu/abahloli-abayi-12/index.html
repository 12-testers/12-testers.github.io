Ukuba nama-imeyili ayi-12 ohlwini akwanele. I-Google ilandelela ukusebenza kwangempela.

Emakhasini okushintshana mahhala, abantu bafaka uhlelo ngosuku lokuqala bese belususa. I-Google Play ibona ukungasebenzi bese yenqaba isicelo sakho.

2. Ukusebenzisa Ama-Emulator Noma Ama-Akhawunti Amanga

I-Google ibona kalula ama-emulator nama-IP afanayo. Ukusebenzisa izindlela ezinqamulelayo kungabeka i-akhawunti yakho engozini.

Izimpendulo Ngaphambi Kokuba Uqale

Ulwazi olucacile ngenkonzo yethu yabahloli abayi-12 izinsuku eziyi-14.

Ingabe le nkonzo ihambisana nemithetho ye-Google Play?
Yebo. I-Google idinga abantu bangempela abahlola uhlelo lwakho kumafoni wangempela izinsuku eziyi-14. Abahloli bethu bangabantu bangempela abajoyina ngesixhumanisi sakho esisemthethweni.
Kungani ninikeza abahloli abayi-25+ esikhundleni sika-12 nje?
Ukuze uvikeleke. Uma umhloli oyedwa eshintsha ifoni noma eba nenkinga yenethiwekhi, inani liyohlala lingaphezu kuka-12 njalo.

Kulula kakhulu. Engeza i-Google Group yethu noma uhlu lwe-imeyili esikunika lona esigabeni sokuhlolwa okuvaliwe ku-Play Console.

Ingabe ningahlola uhlelo oludinga ukungena (Login)?
Yebo. Sinikeze i-akhawunti yokuhlola (demo login) noma abahloli bethu bazobhalisa ngama-imeyili abo.

Sihlola isizathu esinikezwe yi-Google, sikusize ulungise, futhi siphinde ukuhlolwa mahhala — noma sikubuyisele imali yakho engu-100%.
